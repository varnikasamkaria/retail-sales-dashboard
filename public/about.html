About the Project

Retail Sales and Inventory Intelligence

Problem statement

A retail chain with many stores, product categories and customer tiers generates a large number of sales records. Without analysis, it is hard to know which stores, products and discount levels really produce profit, how sales change through the year, and where the business should act. Managers need a clean, reliable and easy-to-read view of sales and profit to make good decisions.

Project objectives

  • Collect and clean the retail sales data so it is accurate and consistent.
  • Calculate revenue, profit, cost, order count, average order value and profit margin.
  • Analyse performance by month, store, product category, membership tier and product.
  • Study how discounts relate to profit margin.
  • Build an interactive dashboard with filters.
  • Write business insights and recommendations from the results.
