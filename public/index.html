About the project

Retail businesses record thousands of transactions, but the numbers only help when they are cleaned, organised and shown clearly. This project takes … sales records from … stores and turns them into an interactive dashboard that answers simple questions: What are we earning? Where is profit made or lost? Which stores, categories and products matter most? How do discounts and seasons affect results?

The project is called Retail Sales and Inventory Intelligence. It covers the sales, pricing, product and supplier details found in the data. Note: the data file has no stock-level (units in stock) columns, so this website does not analyse stock levels.

Objectives

🎯

Measure performance

Track revenue, profit, orders, average order value and profit margin.

🔍

Business value

  • Clarity: one place to see sales, cost and profit.
  • Better pricing decisions: see how discount levels relate to profit margin.
  • Smarter planning: spot strong and weak months to plan staff, supplier orders and promotions.
  • Fair comparison: compare stores, categories and customer tiers on the same measures.
